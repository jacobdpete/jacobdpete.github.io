Floor Income, sometimes called FI or “Floor” was the system of universal basic income implemented in the [[Second Rosen Commonwealth]] from 1995 to 2036.
FI guaranteed a stipend to all Rosen citizens, paying each citizen a fixed amount of credits every month. When the system was reorganized in 2036, Rosens received about AC40 every day or about AC14,600 per year. 
Floor Income was instituted in 1995, during the process of [[Incorporation]]. It was primarily intended to compensate for the lack of material [[Constitution of the Second Rosen Commonwealth#Title 1 Rights, Freedoms, and Obligations of the Citizen|Title 1 Rights]]. Because a private market for housing still existed, and guaranteed food had yet to come to fruition beyond MREs and "flour rations[^1]," guaranteed income allowed Rosens to interact with the private market and meet a basic standard of living.

[^1]: "Flour rations" was a colloquial term for various basic ingredients provided somewhat infrequently through community centers. They technically filled the sustenance guarantee in Title 1, but the judiciary ruled in *Martucelle v. The Commonwealth* that "flour rations" did not meet the sustenance guarantee.
