The Navarro Motor Corporation, or NMC, was a [[Rosen Republic|Rosen]] automobile manufacturer. At its height in 1955, it employed over 200,000 employees. The company was notable for being reasonably open to unions, and several unions existed within NMC. However, in the 1960s, it was revealed several NMC-sanctioned unions were led by [[Sayan Union|Sayan]]-backed communists, some of which were actually spies for the Sayan Union. 
This revelation was highly publicized in the paranoid Republic, resulting in a massive stock sell-off that put the company on the brink of bankruptcy. Eric Marques, president of the Rosen Republic at the time, refused to offer federal money as a bailout, leading to NMC's bankruptcy in May 1961.
The bankruptcy of NMC is often considered a direct cause of the Rosen Republic's decline, and served as a catalyst for rural anarchy in the Republic.
