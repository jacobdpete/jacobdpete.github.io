In the [[Rosen Republic]], the president was the most powerful elected official, wielding almost total control over the executive branch and the federal bureaucracy. Presidents were elected by Rosen citizens for four-year terms. In the First Republic, they could serve an unlimited number of terms. In the Second and Third Republics, they were limited to four terms.FF

# First Republic
**Major parties**
- National Democratic Party
	- nationalism
	- social conservatism
	- pro-business and pro-labor wings
	- heir to confederalists
	- big republican governments
- Liberal Party
	- globalism
	- social conservatism, with a socially progressive wing
	- aggressively pro-business
	- limited government
- Industrial Labor Party
	- nationalism
	- social conservative and progressive wings
	- aggressively pro-labor
	- big government

# Second Republic
**Major parties:**
- Liberal Party
	- globalism
	- social conservatism, with a socially progressive wing
	- aggressively pro-business
	- limited government
- Labor Party
	- nationalism
	- social progressivism
	- aggressively pro-labor, leftist-tolerant
	- big government
- Federal Republican Party
	- moderate on basically everything
	- globalism
- National Party
	- aggressively nationalist
	- majority supremacism (sennans and hanans)
	- big government with small government wing
	- ostensibly pro-labor, but aggressively anti-leftist
- National Rejuvenation Front
	- nationalism
	- social progressivism (civil rights)
	- economic and political reformism
	- big government
	- anti-leftist
