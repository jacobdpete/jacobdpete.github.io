# Colonialism
- 1603: Admiral Amand Lussier makes landfall in East Aya, becoming the first Sayan to step foot on the continet. He establishes the colony of New Avetoix, beginning the record of foreign settlement in East Aya.
- 1605: The Sayan Empire, in the midst of a brutal war of attrition with the Kingdom of Andolina, purchases New Avetoix and grant charters for resource extraction in East Aya.
- 1792: The Winter Revolution: colonists and indigenous East Ayans, calling themselves Rosens, ally to expel the Sayans from East Aya.
- 1793: Congress of Wallaceburg: prominent Sennans organize to form a federal "republic of republics," with a strong collective presidency.
# [[Rosen Federal Union]]
## First System
- 1793: Cecilio Ramos becomes president, pushing rapid industrialization and modernization, instituting a postal system, infrastructural development, and urban expansion.
- 1806: Cesar Ruiz takes power, continuing the trend of industrialization but instituting high tariffs and strict protectionist policies.
- 1821: The Philanthropic Resolution: wealthy Sennans, worried about revolting workers, urge the Red Council to institute democratic reforms, including universal male suffrage.
- 1824: Battle of Navarro: pro-democracy farm workers, including minority sharecroppers, storm and burn the Sennan legislature building, facing resistance from local militias organized by wealthy farmers.
- 1829: Ruiz resigns, backing a proposed charter to overhaul the federal government, which passes.
## Second System
- 1829: The Second Charter replaces the First Charter, instituting universal male suffrage for elections, abolishing poll taxes and literacy tests, and banning slavery in unorganized territories.
- 1830: Hermínio Fidalgo takes power, looking to expand the power of the Red Council, the ever-powerful Rosen collective presidency.
- 1833: Fidalgo issues the Rosen Contract, a memorandum supporting Rosen civic nationalism and the need for less animosity among Sennans, Hanans, and Velese.
- 1836: Fidalgo directs militias to take part in ethnic cleansing in the Rosen plains and far South, despite promises to respect indigenous treaties.
- 1841: Fidalgo is briefly challenged by another Red Council member, Filip Szymkowiak, but Fidalgo reveals a sex scandal that results in his impeachment, becoming the first major scandal in the Rosen government.
- 1842: A newspaper, reporting on the Szymkowiak scandal, is censored by the Red Council, but the censorship is ordered unlawful by the Supreme Court, establishing both judicial review and freedom of the press.
- 1844: The Supreme Court attempts to impeach Fidalgo for breaching freedom of the press and violating the powers of the collective presidency, but Fidalgo uses militias to depose the Court, leaving it vacant.
- 1849: Sennan women advocating for suffrage are attacked by local militias in Tuyei.
- 1849: Tuyei Militia Crisis: Federal army regiments in Seaboard republics conflict with militias over military authority, resulting in Fidalgo dissolving local militias and enshrining the monopoly on violence in the federal government.
- 1856: Gold is discovered in the central plains, resulting in a massive migration west, causing an economic crisis in emptied-out Seaboard cities and further ethnic cleansing in the plains.
- 1857: Fidalgo uses the military to conquer the rest of the plains, "organizing" the territory from ocean to ocean; the campaign is unsuccessful due to a lack of equipment and a massive alliance of indigenous tribes.
- 1857: Fidalgo steps down and puts his brother, Joao Fidalgo, in his place; Joao is seen as untrustworthy, incapable, and corrupt, and so Seaboard republics, excluding Sasei, secede from the republic.
# [[First Rosen Civil War]]
- 1858: Socialists in the northern Seaboard cities secede, with leftist power centered in São Cedillo; the rest of Sasei is held by pro-Council "Reds," other factions call themselves "confederalists"; a three-sided civil war is at play.
- 1859: Battle of Arraias: Confederalists attempt to capture a strategic Red supply line, Socialists approach from behind and make a serious dent in confederalist manpower, Reds win.
- 1860: Battle of Combinado: Reds attempt to surround Socialist stronghold in São Cedillo, Confederalists blockade Socialists but inadvertently choke out Reds from foreign support; Confederalists win, Socialists survive.
- 1862: Armando Ribeiro, commander of Confederalist forces, meets with Adrian Koń, commander of Socialist forces, in an attempt to form an alliance, falls through but tacit agreements are made to focus on defeating the Reds first.
- 1864: Plains Campaign: Reds attempt to invade the plains republics, but face resistance from Socialist-allied indigenous groups
