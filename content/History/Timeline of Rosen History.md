# Colonialism

- **1603** Admiral Amand Lussier makes landfall in East Aya, becoming the first Sayan to step foot on the continent. He establishes the colony of New Avetoix, beginning the record of foreign settlement in East Aya.
- **1605** The Sayan Empire, in the midst of a brutal war of attrition with the Kingdom of Andolina, purchases New Avetoix and grants charters for resource extraction in East Aya.
- **1792** **The Winter Revolution:** Colonists and indigenous East Ayans, calling themselves Rosens, ally to expel the Sayans from East Aya.
- **1793** **Congress of Wallaceburg:** Prominent Sennans organize to form a federal "republic of republics," with a strong collective presidency.
# [[Rosen Federal Union]]

## First System

- **1793** Cecilio Ramos becomes president, pushing rapid industrialization and modernization, instituting a postal system, infrastructural development, and urban expansion.
- **1806** Cesar Ruiz takes power, continuing the trend of industrialization but instituting high tariffs and strict protectionist policies.
- **1821** **The Philanthropic Resolution:** Wealthy Sennans, worried about revolting workers, urge the Red Council to institute democratic reforms, including universal male suffrage.
- **1824** **Battle of Navarro:** Pro-democracy farm workers, including minority sharecroppers, storm and burn the Sennan legislature building, facing resistance from local militias organized by wealthy farmers.
- **1829** Ruiz resigns, backing a proposed charter to overhaul the federal government, which passes.
## Second System

- **1829** The Second Charter replaces the First Charter, instituting universal male suffrage for elections, abolishing poll taxes and literacy tests, and banning slavery in unorganized territories.
- **1830** Hermínio Fidalgo takes power, looking to expand the power of the Red Council, the ever-powerful Rosen collective presidency.
- **1833** Fidalgo issues the **Rosen Contract**, a memorandum supporting Rosen civic nationalism and the need for less animosity among Sennans, Hanans, and Velese.
- **1836** Fidalgo directs militias to take part in ethnic cleansing in the Rosen plains and far South, despite promises to respect indigenous treaties.
- **1841** Fidalgo is briefly challenged by another Red Council member, Filip Szymkowiak, but Fidalgo reveals a sex scandal that results in his impeachment, becoming the first major scandal in the Rosen government.
- **1842** A newspaper, reporting on the Szymkowiak scandal, is censored by the Red Council, but the censorship is ordered unlawful by the Supreme Court, establishing both judicial review and freedom of the press.
- **1844** The Supreme Court attempts to impeach Fidalgo for breaching freedom of the press and violating the powers of the collective presidency, but Fidalgo uses militias to depose the Court, leaving it vacant.
- **1849** Sennan women advocating for suffrage are attacked by local militias in Tuyei.
- **1849** **Tuyei Militia Crisis:** Federal army regiments in Seaboard republics conflict with militias over military authority, resulting in Fidalgo dissolving local militias and enshrining the monopoly on violence in the federal government.
- **1856** Gold is discovered in the central plains, resulting in a massive migration west, causing an economic crisis in emptied-out Seaboard cities and further ethnic cleansing in the plains.
- **1857** Fidalgo uses the military to conquer the rest of the plains, "organizing" the territory from ocean to ocean; the campaign is unsuccessful due to a lack of equipment and a massive alliance of indigenous tribes.
- **1857** Fidalgo steps down and puts his brother, Joao Fidalgo, in his place; Joao is seen as untrustworthy, incapable, and corrupt, and so Seaboard republics, excluding Sasei, secede from the republic.
# [[First Rosen Civil War]]

- **1858** Socialists in the northern Seaboard cities secede, with leftist power centered in **[[São Cedillo]]**; the rest of Sasei is held by pro-Council "Reds," while other factions call themselves "Confederalists"—a three-sided civil war is at play.
- **1859** **Battle of Arraias:** Confederalists attempt to capture a strategic Red supply line; Socialists approach from behind and make a serious dent in Confederalist manpower. Reds win.
- **1860** **Battle of Combinado:** Reds attempt to surround the Socialist stronghold in **[[São Cedillo]]**; Confederalists blockade the Socialists but inadvertently choke out the Reds from foreign support. Confederalists win, Socialists survive.
- **1862** **[[Armando Ribeiro]]**, commander of Confederalist forces, meets with Adrian Koń, commander of Socialist forces, in an attempt to form an alliance; it falls through, but tacit agreements are made to focus on defeating the Reds first.
- **1864** **Plains Campaign:** Reds attempt to invade the plains republics, but face resistance from Socialist-allied indigenous groups; Socialists eventually create an exclave in the plains, controlling agriculture.
- **1865** **[[Armando Ribeiro|Ribeiro]]** breaks his informal agreement with Koń, attacking the Socialists in the plains in Vazante; Socialists win, but not unscathed.
- **1866** Starving Confederalist leadership forces Ribeiro to meet with Koń, resulting in the **Patriotic Alliance**; Confederalists and Socialists create a formal alliance and coordinate a push against the Reds.
- **1868** **Battle of São Flores:** Reds are nearly wiped out in an attack on **[[São Cedillo]]** suburbs; over several months, PA forces push Reds further inland and take their command seat in the São Cedillo outskirts.
- **1870** **Battle of Nova Ponte:** Disgruntled Confederalists attack Socialists in the plains; Ribeiro goes along with it and dissolves the PA.
- **1871** Confederalists, now in control of agriculture, starve the Socialists and Reds into submission, resulting in the surrender of the Reds in March and an armistice with the Socialists in October.
- **1871** **[[Concordance of São Lola]]:** Confederalists and Socialists meet in São Lola, on the Leeward Coast, to reform the **[[Rosen Federal Union]]**; Confederalists push for a new state, the **[[Rosen Republic]]**, with a weaker presidency and a bicameral legislature. Confederalists lead the conference and Socialist forces are pushed to disarm and dissolve.
# [[Rosen Republic]]
## First Republic
- **1871** The Rosen Republic is founded, with Vincenzo Salera, a relatively unknown democrat, as provisional president; Ribeiro angles for a military-backed provisional government, faces opposition from industrial delegates who back Maurycy Basara. Ribeiro becomes commander-in-chief.
- **1872** Salera remains president, and puts Basara and Ribeiro in charge of drafting a constitution; they come up with the [[New Articles of Association for the Rosen Republic|New Articles]], a temporary bill of rights finally establishing freedom of speech, press, and assembly.
- **1875** The constitution, after three years of bickering and deadlock, is finally promulgated, with national presidential elections scheduled for January 1876.
- **1876** Salera wins reelection, but resigns at the end of the year due to to a bribery scandal. His vice president, popular industrialist Joaquim Neves-Coelho, becomes president at the end of the year.
- **1879** Sasei River Railway merges with Northeast Lines, becoming Northeast Passenger Railways (NPR), the first major rail company in the Republic.
- **1881** Coelho passes the Deregulation Acts, a package of laws deregulating rapidly-growing industries such as railways, real estate, steelmaking, oil extraction, and banking.
- **1884** Rosen National Bank acquires First Vian Bank becoming the largest bank in the Republic; CEO Lúcio Queiroz becomes the first multi-millionaire.
- **1885** **The Great Strike**: A general strike is declared by the Industrial Labor Union (ILU), resulting in a week of riots and counter-strikes; Coelho's response is deemed weak and he resigns; He is replaced by Leon Podolak, president of the São Cedillo Chamber of Commerce.
- **1889** Veli territory is divided into the Veli, Selya, and Fevei Republics; The entire Rosen mainland is "organized."
- **1893** Leon Podolak fails to win a third term; Vítor Maldonado de Assis, known as VMA, a prominent trade unionist and reformer, narrowly becomes president.
- **1895** National Petrochemical becomes the largest company in the world by market capitalization; The same year, strike breakers attack Velese conservationists, resulting in the Assis administration investigating the company.
- **1896** **National Petrochemical v. Rosen Republic:** National Petrochemical is ordered by the Department of Commerce to split up, it refuses; the courts rule in National's favor; inadvertently establishes corporate personhood.
- **1897** Assis, in the last year of his term, creates a first-of-its-kind social security system for the Republic.
