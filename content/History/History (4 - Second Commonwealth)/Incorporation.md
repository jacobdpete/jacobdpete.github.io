Incorporation was the process by which the [[Second Rosen Commonwealth]] absorbed polities formed after the [[Nuclear Crisis]]. Most historians agree it began after the [[Constitution of the Second Rosen Commonwealth|Second Constitution]] went into effect in November 1990, and ended with the incorporation of the Veytuan Councils into the Veytu Republic in 2002. Incorporation was vital to legitimizing the Commonwealth's existence after the upheaval of the Crisis.
