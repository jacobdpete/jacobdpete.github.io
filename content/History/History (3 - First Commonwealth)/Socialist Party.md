The Socialist Party, officially the Worker's Socialist Party of the Rosen Commonwealth (WSPRC) was the ruling party in the [[First Rosen Commonwealth]] and ran the country as a one-party state. The party suffered from debilitating internal conflicts and is often regarded as a primary reason for the First Commonwealth's collapse.
Initially led by the Crisis Politburo, the WSPRC was dominated by its military wing, the People's Army, which controlled decision-making on the politburo until well after the [[Second Rosen Civil War|Second Civil War]] ended in August 1983. Conflict between parallel hierarchies of power in the Army and the Party resulted in Julian Rybicki, with the backing of prominent socialists such as [[Jorge Almeida]], [[Hatsue Yuhara]], and Deng Liang assisting in his path to power. Elected General Secretary in September, Rybicki immediately turned against Almeida's moderates. Disobeying a binding vote of no confidence from the People's Congress, Rybicki successfully led a self-coup and imprisoned or silenced socialists with agendas different to his. 
The WSPRC was a primary motivation for [[ICOSGEA]]'s formation, as the countries in West Aya believed the Commonwealth would start a new series of interventionist conflicts under the Party's rule.
