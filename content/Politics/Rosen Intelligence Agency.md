The Rosen Intelligence Agency, RIA, is the federal intelligence service of the [[Rosen Commonwealth]], tasked with maintaining and advancing national security interests through collecting and analyzing intelligence around the world. RIA is headquartered in Świlona, Kana, but the agency maintains a sprawling apparatus of satellite organizations across the Commonwealth and the world. 
It is often said that RIA has penetrated state apparatuses in the Commonwealth's adversaries, notably [[Saya]] and the [[West Ayan Republic]], so deeply that the Commonwealth can actually play a major role in the day-to-day operations of said countries. At home, RIA maintains several installations to aid in counterintelligence, although a [[Bełnowiec Times]] investigative program uncovered that RIA had violated Rosen privacy laws in its investigation of Oliwia Joniłowicz, herself an investigative journalist.
As an independent agency, RIA does not report to the Department of Defense or the [[Senate]], instead reporting directly to the [[Secretariat-Directory]]. The current Director of RIA is Antonio Pellerito.
