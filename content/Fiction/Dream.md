i slept terribly, but what a dream!
i was a bird, flying along the leeward coast. it was dusk and the fog was rolling back inland. you know what it does when the fog goes right up to the coast and ceases like it’s hit a wall? 
it was like that, and demei—navarro. navarro was incredible. strung like a gold necklace along the coast. with the five centers up against the hills. 
yes, yes, and the highways splayed out like webs into the continent. graceful silver lines to the next golden necklace.
it was not to be, though. navarro was obscured by the fog. i could no longer see my dream. i could feel—not hear—thunder along the hills, yet there was no lightning. demei’s absence revealed man’s presence. bombs. fire. *está morto*. 

and now navarro is gone. i haven’t dreamt since.
