The engines he will have invented will be beyond his strength to control. Someday science may have the existence of mankind in its power, and the human race commit suicide, by blowing up the world.*

If we want to be awfully melodramatic about it, the Commonwealth is built on a sea of corpses. About 1 to 200 million, to be exact. 
This bit of disjointed commentary serves to argue that the Nuclear Crisis serves as the Second Rosen Commonwealth's - and perhaps the Avaya Project as a whole's - original sin.
# Discussion

> *In Christian theology, original sin is the condition of sinfulness that all humans share, which they inherit from the Fall of Adam and Eve, involving the loss of original righteousness and the distortion of the Image of God.*
> 
> Wikipedia

In other words, there is an inherited bit of wrongdoing that all of humanity cannot escape, requiring constant atonement. This is what the Commonwealth is experiencing, all the time. Let's get the ball rolling: the Second Commonwealth literally can't exist without the Nuclear Crisis. The First Commonwealth probably would have collapsed into civil war or anarchy at some point or another, but it certainly wouldn't have happened as fast as it did without the Crisis.
Then, the Commonwealth was founded by the people who experienced this firsthand: coming off the heels of the betrayal that was the founding of the First Commonwealth, they then experienced nuclear armageddon in a span of a decade-ish.
