The Bełnowiec Times is a regional newspaper in the Hana Republic and surrounding areas. It is famous for its 2042 investigation into the [[Rosen Intelligence Agency]], which revealed violations of the privacy rights of investigative journalist Oliwia Joniłowicz. On the other hand, it is a major authority on gastronomy, especially in the Northern Seaboard. The "Best Lobster Award" is highly prestigious and only awarded every ten years.
