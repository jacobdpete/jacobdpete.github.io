The [[Rosen Commonwealth]] was created on Google Docs, where I did most of my worldbuilding work at the time. I experimented with Obsidian, a markdown text editor that stores files locally. Migrating my Commonwealth stuff there led to an explosion of development, especially because of Obsidian's emphasis on linking articles.
Inspired by the MillMint website, I discovered Quartz, a static-site generator that enabled me to turn my Obsidian vault into a digital garden or public wiki of sorts. Github Pages lets me host the site for free!

Icons are from W3.css.
"Swansea" is the font used for the website, created by Roger White.
