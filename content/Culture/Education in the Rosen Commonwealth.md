Education is held in high regard in the [[Rosen Commonwealth]]: it is one of the public sector's largest employers, it is critical to the Commonwealth's social, political, and economic success, and it is offered at scale for no charge. Education is compulsory from ages six to nineteen, split into two blocks: lower grades (first to sixth grade), a year-long break, and upper grades (seventh to twelfth grade). Higher education is optional but common; about 66% of Rosens have college degrees. Higher education is administered through the Commonwealth's many colleges and universities, many of which are constituents of the federal [[National University]] system.
